00.03.1 | Serve Agni with the kindling-stick; awaken the guest with ghee; into him offer the oblations.
00.03.2 | To the well-kindled flame offer sharp ghee, to Agni Jātavedas.
00.03.3 | You, O Aṅgiras, we make grow with kindling-sticks and with ghee; blaze high, O youngest one.
00.03.4 | Let the oblation-bearing, ghee-dripping ones go to you, O Agni, O delightful one; accept my kindling-sticks.
00.03.5 | Bhūḥ, bhuvaḥ, svaḥ! Like the sky in greatness, like the earth in width: O Earth, O place of worship of the gods, on the back of you, that one, I set Agni the food-eater, for the eating of food.
00.03.6 | This dappled bull has come; he has sat down before the mother, and going forth to the father, to heaven.
00.03.7 | The shining one moves within, breathing out from his breath; the mighty one has beheld the sky.
00.03.8 | It shines over thirty domains; speech is set for the bird; at each dawning, indeed, with the days.
00.03.9a | Agni is light, light is Agni, svāhā. Sūrya is light, light is Sūrya, svāhā. Agni is splendour, light is splendour, svāhā. Sūrya is splendour, light is splendour, svāhā. Light is Sūrya, Sūrya is light, svāhā.

00.03.10a | Together with the god Savitṛ, together with Night accompanied by Indra, may Agni, accepting, enjoy, svāhā. Together with the god Savitṛ, together with Dawn accompanied by Indra, may Sūrya, accepting, enjoy, svāhā.
00.03.10b | ~
00.03.11 | Approaching the rite, may we speak a mantra to Agni, who hears afar and among us.
00.03.12 | Agni is the head of heaven, the summit; this one is the lord of the earth; he quickens the seeds of the waters.
00.03.13a | Both of you, Indra and Agni, to invoke, both to delight together in the bounty; both the givers of refreshments and of riches, both of you I call for the winning of strength.
00.03.13b | ~
00.03.14 | This is your timely seat, from which, born, you shone; knowing it, O Agni, mount it; then make our wealth grow.
00.03.15a | This one was here set first by the establishers as the hotṛ, best at sacrifice, to be praised at the rites; whom Apnavāna and the Bhṛgus made shine in the woods, bright, pervading, for every clan.
00.03.15b | ~
00.03.16 | Following his ancient brilliance, the bold ones have milked the bright milk, the thousand-winning sage.
00.03.17a | You are the body-protector, O Agni; protect my body. You are the life-giver, O Agni; give me life. You are the splendour-giver, O Agni; give me splendour. O Agni, what is lacking in my body, fill that up for me.
00.03.17b | ~
00.03.18a | Kindling you for a hundred winters, may we, full of vigour, full of might and unharmed, kindle you, O Agni: the brilliant one, the vigour-maker, the might-maker, the crusher of rivals, the one who cannot be harmed. O you of bright wealth, with your well-being may I reach the far shore.
00.03.18b | ~
00.03.19a | You, O Agni, have united with the splendour of Sūrya, with the praise of the sages; may I unite with the dear abode, I with life, with splendour, with offspring, with increase of wealth.
00.03.19b | ~
00.03.20 | You are food; may I partake of your food. You are greatness; may I partake of your greatness. You are vigour; may I partake of your vigour. You are increase of wealth; may I partake of your increase of wealth.
00.03.21 | O rich ones, abide in this seat, in this cow-pen, in this world, in this dwelling; stay right here; do not go away.
00.03.22a | You are united, of all forms; with vigour enter into me, with lordship over cows. To you, O Agni, day by day, O illuminer of darkness, we come with prayer, bearing homage.
00.03.22b | ~
00.03.23 | Ruling over the rites, the shining guardian of truth, growing in his own house.
00.03.24 | As a father to a son, O Agni, be easy of access to us; stay with us for well-being.
00.03.25a | O Agni, be our nearest one and a protector, kindly and sheltering; Agni is good, of good renown; come hither; give most brilliant wealth.
00.03.25b | ~
00.03.26a | You, O most brilliant, O shining one, we now beseech for favour, for the friends; take notice of us, hear our call, protect us from every one who seeks to harm.
00.03.26b | ~
00.03.27 | O Iḍā, come; O Aditi, come; O desirable ones, come. May the holding of your desires be in me.
00.03.28 | O Brahmaṇaspati, make the Soma-presser resounding, Kakṣīvat who is the son of Uśij (read as apposition or as a comparison; see note).
00.03.29 | He who is wealthy, who is the disease-slayer, the wealth-finder, the increaser of prosperity, who is swift, let him attend us.
00.03.30 | Let not the curse of the hostile one, nor the malice of a mortal, reach us; protect us, O Brahmaṇaspati.
00.03.31 | Great be the help of the three, heavenly, of Mitra and of Aryaman, hard to assail, of Varuṇa.
00.03.32 | For neither at home nor on dangerous roads does the evil-speaking enemy have power over them.
00.03.33 | For they, the sons of Aditi, bestow unceasing light on the mortal, that he may live.
00.03.34 | You are never barren, O Indra; you attend the worshipper who gives. Nearer and nearer still, O Maghavan, and more: the god's gift of yours is bestowed.
00.03.35 | We meditate on that excellent radiance of the god Savitṛ, who may impel our thoughts.
00.03.36 | May your chariot, hard to harm, encompass us on all sides, by which you protect the worshippers who give.
00.03.37a | Bhūḥ, Bhuvaḥ, Svaḥ. May I be rich in offspring through offspring, rich in heroes through heroes, well nourished through nourishments. O manly one, protect my offspring; O praiseworthy one, protect my cattle; O atharya, protect my food.
00.03.37b | ~
00.03.38 | We have come to the all-possessing one, the best finder of wealth for us. O Agni, sovereign, extend to us splendour and might.
00.03.39 | This Agni, the house-lord, the Gārhapatya, is the best finder of wealth for offspring. O Agni, house-lord, extend to us splendour and might.
00.03.40 | This Agni, the Purīṣya, rich in wealth, increaser of prosperity. O Agni, O Purīṣya, extend to us splendour and might.
00.03.41 | O houses, do not fear, do not tremble; bearing vigour we come. Bearing vigour for you, well-disposed, of good understanding, I come to the houses, rejoicing in mind.
00.03.42 | We invite the houses which the one living abroad remembers, in which there is abundant goodwill; let them, knowing, know us.
00.03.43 | Invited here are the cows; invited are the goats and sheep; and the sweet essence of food is invited in our houses. For security and for peace I resort to you: the auspicious, the helpful, welfare, welfare.
00.03.44 | We invoke the Praghāsins, and the Maruts, the devourers of foes, in accord with the gruel (karambha).
00.03.45 | Whatever sin we have committed in the village, in the forest, in the assembly, in the faculty, that we here sacrifice away. Svāhā.
00.03.46a | O Indra, do not (leave) us here in the battles; for with the gods there is indeed your share (avayā), O mighty one. Of whom, the great and bountiful one, the song of the one with oblation praises the Maruts.
00.03.46b | ~
00.03.47 | The doers of the rite have performed the rite together with joy-bringing speech. Having performed the rite for the gods, go home, O companions.
00.03.48a | O Avabhṛtha, O Nicumpuṇa, you are Nicera, Nicumpuṇa. With the gods I have removed the sin done by the gods; with mortals, the sin done by mortals. O god, of Pururāvan, protect (me) from harm.
00.03.48b | ~
00.03.49 | O full ladle, fly away; well filled, fly back again. As if for a price, let us two exchange food and strength, O hundred-powered one.
00.03.50 | Give to me, I give to you; place for me, I place for you. And may you carry off the nihāra for me, may I carry off the nihāra for you. Svāhā.
00.03.51a | They have eaten, they have rejoiced; the dear ones have shaken themselves off. The self-radiant inspired ones have praised with the newest hymn. Now, O Indra, yoke your two bay horses.
00.03.51b | ~
00.03.52a | May we praise you, O Maghavan, beautiful to behold. Praised, O you of the full chariot-seat, you go forth now according to (our) wishes. Now, O Indra, yoke your two bay horses.
00.03.52b | ~
00.03.53 | We now call back the mind with the Nārāśaṃsa praise-song and with the thoughts of the Fathers.
00.03.54 | May the mind come back to us again, for resolve, for ability, for living, and to see the sun for long.
00.03.55 | O Fathers, may the divine host give us the mind again; may we join the living company.
00.03.56 | O Soma, in your ordinance, bearing the mind in our bodies, may we, possessed of offspring, attend.
00.03.57 | This is your share, O Rudra, together with the sister Ambikā; enjoy it. Svāhā. This is your share, O Rudra; the mouse is your animal.
00.03.58 | We have appeased Rudra, the god Tryambaka, so that he may make us better off, so that he may make us more excellent, so that he may make us resolute.
00.03.59 | You are a remedy: a remedy for the cow, for the horse, for the man; ease for the ram, for the ewe.
00.03.60a | We sacrifice to Tryambaka, the fragrant, the increaser of prosperity. Like a cucumber from its stalk, may I be freed from death, not from immortality. We sacrifice to Tryambaka, the fragrant, the finder of a husband. Like a cucumber from its stalk, may I be freed from here, not from there.

00.03.62 | Threefold life of Jamadagni, threefold life of Kaśyapa; the threefold life that is among the gods, may that be ours: threefold life.
