00.06.1a | At the impulse of the god Savitṛ, with the arms of the two Aśvins and the hands of Pūṣan, I take you. You are a woman; here I cut off the necks of the demons. You are barley; keep hatreds away from us, keep hostile powers away. You for heaven, you for the atmosphere, you for the earth. May the worlds, the seats of the fathers, be purified. You are the seat of the fathers.

00.06.2a | You are the leader; O you of good entry, take note of this of the unnetṛs. One will stand upon you. May the god Savitṛ anoint you with honey, you for the plants with good fruit. With your tip you touched heaven, with your middle you filled the atmosphere, with your lower part you made the earth firm.

00.06.3a | Those abodes of yours that we desire to reach, where the many-horned nimble cows are: there indeed the highest step of wide-striding Viṣṇu has been abundantly borne down. O you who win priestly power, who win ruling power, who win wealth and prosperity, I place you around. Make firm the priestly power, make firm the ruling power, make firm life, make firm offspring.

00.06.4 | Look at the rites of Viṣṇu, from which he has beheld the observances, Indra's fitting friend.
00.06.5 | The wise ones always see that highest step of Viṣṇu, like an eye spread out in the sky.
00.06.6a | You are the encircler. May the divine clans wrap around you, and around this sacrificer the riches of men. You are the son of heaven. This is your world on the earth. The wild animal is your animal.
00.06.6b | ~
00.06.7 | You are the one who approaches; the divine clans, the eager ones, went forth to the gods, the best bearers. O god Tvaṣṭṛ, take delight in the good things; may the offerings be sweet to you.
00.06.8 | O wealthy ones, take delight; O Bṛhaspati, hold the goods. O offering of the gods, I fasten you with the cord of order. Dharṣā, the human.
00.06.9 | At the impulse of the god Savitṛ, with the arms of the two Aśvins and the hands of Pūṣan, I yoke you, pleasing to Agni and Soma. You for the waters, you for the plants. May the mother consent to you, the father, the brother of the same womb, the friend of the same herd. I sprinkle you, pleasing to Agni and Soma.
00.06.10 | You are the peru of the waters. May the divine waters sweeten the well-eaten offering to the gods, even as it is. May your breath unite with the wind, your limbs with the worshipful ones, and the lord of the sacrifice with the blessing.
00.06.11a | You two, anointed with ghee, protect the animals; O wealthy one, place what is dear in the sacrificer and enter. From the wide atmosphere, in company with the divine wind, sacrifice by yourself of this offering; be one with this one's body. O varṣa, in the greater sacrifice set the lord of the sacrifice. Svāhā to the gods; to the gods svāhā.

00.06.12 | Do not be a snake, do not be a pṛdāku; homage to you. O ātāna, go forth unhindered, along the streams of ghee, along the paths of order.
00.06.13 | O divine waters, pure, carry; well served; among the gods well served, may we be the servers.
00.06.14 | I purify your speech; I purify your breath; I purify your eye; I purify your ear; I purify your navel; I purify your meḍhra; I purify your anus; I purify your means of going.
00.06.15a | May your mind swell fully; may your speech swell fully; may your breath swell fully; may your eye swell fully; may your ear swell fully. What of yours is harsh, what abides, may that swell fully, may it be removed, may that of yours be purified, for well-being, for the days. O herb, protect; O blade, do not harm him.

00.06.16a | You are the share of the demons. The demon is cast out; here I stand upon the demon; here I thrust the demon down; here I lead the demon to the lowest darkness. O heaven and earth, cover yourselves with ghee. O Vāyu, enjoy the drops; let Agni enjoy the clarified butter. Svāhā! O you two made with svāhā, go to the high sky, the Maruts'.

00.06.17a | O waters, carry this away forth, the fault and the dirt, whatever it is; and what I have wronged by untruth, and what I have cursed, abhīruṇa. May the waters and the purifying one release me from that sin.

00.06.18a | May your mind unite with mind, your breath with breath. You are reṭ; let Agni mix you, the waters you. They joined. You for the rush of the wind, for the speed of Pūṣan; the wavering of the heat has happened (uncertain); hatred is separated.

00.06.19a | O drinkers of ghee, drink the ghee; O drinkers of fat, drink the fat. You are the offering of the atmosphere. Svāhā. Directions, pra-directions, ā-directions, vi-directions, ud-directions; svāhā to the directions.

00.06.20a | Indra's breath, may it shine in each limb; Indra's udāna is placed in each limb. O god Tvaṣṭṛ, let much come together for you, of one mark, whatever shall be of diverse form. Let friends, mother and fathers rejoice after you, going among the gods for help.

00.06.21a | Go to the sea, svāhā; go to the atmosphere, svāhā; go to the god Savitṛ, svāhā; go to Mitra and Varuṇa, svāhā. Go to day and night, svāhā; go to the metres, svāhā; go to heaven and earth, svāhā; go to the sacrifice, svāhā; go to Soma, svāhā; go to the divine sky, svāhā; go to Agni Vaiśvānara, svāhā. Hold my mind, that of the heart. Let your smoke go to heaven, your light to svar; fill the earth with ash. Svāhā.

00.06.22a | Do not harm the waters, do not harm the plants. From each abode, O king, from there, O Varuṇa, release us. What they say, 'aghnyā', and 'O Varuṇa!', thus we swear; from that, O Varuṇa, release us. May the waters and plants be friendly to us; may they be unfriendly to him who hates us and whom we hate.

00.06.23 | The one who has the offering serves these waters, which are rich in offerings. May the divine rite be provided with offerings; may Sūrya be provided with offerings.
00.06.24 | In the seat of Agni of the unfallen house I seat you. You are the portions of Indra and Agni; you are the portions of Mitra and Varuṇa; you are the portions of all the gods. Those that are near Sūrya, or with which Sūrya is together, may they speed our rite.
00.06.25 | You for the heart, you for the mind, you for heaven, you for Sūrya. Extend this rite upward, in heaven among the gods, hotrā (form uncertain).
00.06.26a | O Soma, O king, descend upon all creatures; let all creatures descend to you. Let Agni hear my call with the kindling; let the waters and the divine dhiṣaṇās hear. O pressing stones, listen to the sacrifice like those who know; let the god Savitṛ hear my call. Svāhā.

00.06.28 | You are kārṣi. I lead you up for the habitation of the sea. The waters have come together with the waters, the plants with the plants.
00.06.29 | O Agni, the mortal whom you helped in battles, whom you urged on in contests: may he hold lasting refreshments. Svāhā.

00.06.33 | The light of yours, O Soma, that is in heaven, that on earth, that in the wide atmosphere: with that make wide for this sacrificer, for wealth; speak on behalf of the giver.
00.06.34 | You are swift, overcoming vṛtra, welcomed by bounty, the wives of the immortal. O divine ones, lead this sacrifice among the gods; invited, drink of Soma.
00.06.35 | Do not fear, do not tremble; hold vigour. O two dhiṣaṇās, being firm, make firm and hold vigour. Evil is slain, not Soma.
