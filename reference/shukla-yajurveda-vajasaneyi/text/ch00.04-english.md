00.04.1 | We have come here to this place of sacrifice to the gods on the earth, where all the gods took pleasure; crossing over with the Ṛc and Sāman verses and with the yajus formulas, may we rejoice with increase of wealth and refreshment. May these divine waters be wholesome for me. O herb, protect; O blade, do not harm him.
00.04.2a | May the waters, the mothers, purify us; may the purifiers of ghee cleanse us with ghee. For the divine ones carry away all impurity; out of these I come, pure and cleansed. You are the body of dīkṣā and tapas; that kindly, helpful one I put on, nourishing a good colour.

00.04.3 | You are the milk of the great ones; you are the giver of splendour: give me splendour. You are the pupil of Vṛtra; you are the giver of sight: give me sight.
00.04.4a | May the lord of thought purify me; may the lord of speech purify me; may the god Savitṛ purify me with the unbroken strainer, with the rays of Sūrya. O lord of the strainer, of you who are purified by the strainer: with whatever desire I purify, may I be able to achieve that.
00.04.4b | ~
00.04.5 | O gods, we ask of you what is precious, at the rite now proceeding. O gods worthy of sacrifice, we call on you for blessings.
00.04.6 | Hail! The sacrifice from the mind; hail! from the wide mid-region; hail! from heaven and earth; hail! from the wind I take it up; hail!
00.04.7 | Hail to Intention, to Application, to Agni! Hail to Wisdom, to Mind, to Agni! Hail to Dīkṣā, to Tapas, to Agni! Hail to Sarasvatī, to Pūṣan, to Agni! O divine waters, great and all-beneficent, O heaven and earth, O wide mid-region, may we serve Bṛhaspati with the oblation. Hail!
00.04.8 | May every mortal choose the friendship of the leading god; everyone seeks riches and chooses splendour, for thriving. Hail!
00.04.9a | You two are the works of art of the Ṛc and the Sāman. I take hold of you two; protect me, you two, up to the close of this sacrifice. You are shelter; give me shelter. Homage to you; do not harm me.
00.04.9b | ~
00.04.10a | You are strength, belonging to Aṅgiras, soft as wool: place strength in me. You are the wrapper of Soma; you are Viṣṇu's shelter, the sacrificer's shelter; you are the womb of Indra; make the ploughed lands rich in good grain. Rise up, O lord of the forest; standing upright, protect me from distress up to the close of this sacrifice.

00.04.10c | ~
00.04.11a | Perform the observance: Agni is the Brahman priest, Agni is the sacrifice, the lord of the forest is worthy of sacrifice. For our aim we call to mind the divine insight, very gracious, the giver of splendour, the carrier of the sacrifice; may she be of good access and in our power. The gods who are born of the mind, yoked by the mind, of skilful will: may they help us, may they protect us. Hail to them!

00.04.12a | Once drunk, O waters, be quickening and very kind within our belly. May they, the divine, immortal waters that increase the order (ṛta), be pleasing to us, free from wasting sickness, free from illness, faultless.

00.04.13a | This is your sacrificial body; I release the waters, not the offspring. You who free from distress, you who are made with svāhā, enter the earth. Unite with the earth.
00.04.13b | ~
00.04.14 | O Agni, stay well awake; may we rejoice well. Protect us, not neglecting us; at our waking make us anew.
00.04.15a | Again the mind has come to me; again life has come to me; again the breath, again the self has come to me; again sight, again hearing has come to me. May Agni Vaiśvānara, unharmed, the guardian of the body, protect us from misfortune and from blame.
00.04.15b | ~
00.04.16 | O Agni, you are the guardian of observances, a god among mortals; you are to be praised at the sacrifices. Give this much, O Soma; bring more. May our god Savitṛ, the giver of wealth, give wealth.
00.04.17 | This is your body, O bright one; this is splendour; unite with that, go to radiance. You are swift, held by the mind, pleasing to Viṣṇu.
00.04.18 | Of that one, of you whose impulse is true: in the impulse, may I obtain the control of your body. Hail! You are bright; you are shining; you are immortal; you belong to all the gods.
00.04.19a | You are thought, you are mind, you are insight, you are Dakṣiṇā, you are Kṣatriyā, worthy of sacrifice; you are Aditi, with a head at both ends. As such, be well facing forward and well facing back for us. Let Mitra bind you by the foot; let Pūṣan guard the road; for Indra the overseer.
00.04.19b | ~
00.04.20a | Let your mother consent, your father, your brother of the same womb, your friend of the same herd. O goddess, go towards the god, to Soma for Indra. May Rudra turn you back; with Soma as your friend, come back again in well-being.
00.04.20b | ~
00.04.21 | You are the wealthy one, you are Aditi, you belong to the Ādityas, you belong to the Rudras, you are shining. May Bṛhaspati delight you in favour; Rudra, with the Vasus, has taken pleasure.
00.04.22a | On the head of Aditi, at the place of sacrifice to the gods on the earth, I sprinkle you; you are the place of Iḍā, rich in ghee; hail! Take delight in us; in us is your kinship; in you is wealth, mine is wealth; may we not be parted from increase of wealth; then wealth.
00.04.22b | ~
00.04.23a | I have been joined (or have looked) with the divine insight, with the generous, wide-seeing one. Do not steal my life, nor I yours. May I find a hero in your sight, O goddess.
00.04.23b | ~
00.04.24 | 'This is your Gāyatra share': so say to Soma for me. 'This is your Traiṣṭubha share': so say to Soma for me. 'This is your Jāgata share': so say to Soma for me. 'Go to the sovereignty of those named after the metres': so say to Soma for me. You are ours; your bright cup is to be taken; may the discerning ones sift you.
00.04.25 | I praise that god Savitṛ of the two, seer-willed, of true impulse, bestower of treasure; the dear thought, the seer. His lofty splendour, his light, shone in the impulse; the golden-handed, of good skill, measured out heaven by his grace. For the creatures (I take) you; let the creatures breathe after you; you breathe after the creatures.
00.04.26 | I buy you, the bright one, with the bright; the shining with the shining; the immortal with the immortal. In sagme (obscure) your cow; in us your shining things. You are the body of tapas, the colour of Prajāpati; you are bought with the highest. May I thrive in a thousandfold increase.
00.04.27 | Mitra, come to us, establisher of good friendship; enter the wide right side of Indra, desiring towards the desiring, agreeable towards the agreeable. O Svāna, Bhrāja, Aṅghāri, Bambhāri, Hasta, Suhasta, Kṛśānu: these are your Soma-purchase (things); guard them; let none harm you.
00.04.28 | Drive me away from ill conduct, O Agni; assign me to good conduct. With life, with good life, I have risen up, after the immortals.
00.04.29 | We have stepped onto the path that leads to well-being and is free from harm, by which one avoids all enemies and finds wealth.
00.04.30a | You are the skin of Aditi; sit upon the seat of Aditi. The bull propped up heaven, measured out the mid-region and the breadth of the earth. The universal ruler sat down upon all the worlds; all those are indeed Varuṇa's ordinances.
00.04.30b | ~
00.04.31 | Varuṇa stretched out the mid-region over the forests; he placed strength in the horses, milk in the cows, will in the hearts, fire among the peoples, Sūrya in the sky, Soma on the rock.
00.04.32 | Ascend the eye of Sūrya, the pupil of the eye of Agni, where you move, shining, with the bay horses, with the inspired one.
00.04.33 | O two oxen, come; bearing the yoke, be yoked, tearless, not slaying men, urging on the sacred word; go in well-being to the houses of the sacrificer.
00.04.34 | You are auspicious to me. Go forth, O lord of the world, over all places. Let not the waylayers find you; let not the highwaymen find you; let not the wolves bent on harm find you. Having become a falcon, fly away; go to the houses of the sacrificer; that is prepared for us two.
00.04.35 | Homage to the eye of Mitra and Varuṇa, to the great god; honour that order. To the far-seeing, god-born banner, the son of heaven, to Sūrya, recite praise.
00.04.36a | You are the support of Varuṇa; you two are Varuṇa's skambhasarjanī; you are Varuṇa's seat of order; you are Varuṇa's seat of order. Sit down in Varuṇa's seat of order.

00.04.37a | Those abodes of yours which they worship with oblation: may you, encompassing all those of yours, encompass the sacrifice. Increasing the household, bringing safe passage, rich in heroes, not slaying heroes, go forth, O Soma, to the houses.
