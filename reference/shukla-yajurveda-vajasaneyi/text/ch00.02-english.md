00.02.1 | You are black, standing in the lair; dear to Agni, I sprinkle you. You are the altar (vedi); dear to the barhis (sacrificial grass), I sprinkle you. You are the barhis; dear to the ladles, I sprinkle you.
00.02.2a | You are the moistening for Aditi. O Viṣṇu, you are the crest (stupa). Soft as wool, a good seat for the gods, I spread you. To the lord of the earth, svāhā; to the lord of the world, svāhā; to the lord of beings, svāhā.
00.02.2b | ~
00.02.3 | May the Gandharva Viśvāvasu lay you around, for the safety of all, of the sacrificer; you are an enclosing-stick; Agni is praised by Iḍā. You are Indra's right arm, for the safety of all, of the sacrificer; you are an enclosing-stick; Agni is praised by Iḍā. May Mitra and Varuṇa lay you around on the north with firm dharma, for the safety of all, of the sacrificer; you are an enclosing-stick; Agni is praised by Iḍā.
00.02.4 | You, O seer, the feast-offerer, the bright one, may we kindle; O Agni, the great one, at the rite.
00.02.5 | You are a fuel-stick. May the sun guard you from the east against any curse. You two are Savitṛ's arms. Soft as wool, a good seat for the gods, I spread you. May the Vasus, the Rudras, the Ādityas sit upon you.
00.02.6 | You are ghee-laden, Juhū by name; sit here, she, with your dear abode upon the dear seat. You are ghee-laden, Upabhṛt by name; sit here, she, with your dear abode upon the dear seat. You are ghee-laden, Dhruvā by name; sit here, she, with your dear abode upon the dear seat. With your dear abode sit upon the dear seat. Firm they have sat down in the womb of order (ṛta); guard them, O Viṣṇu; guard the sacrifice, guard the lord of the sacrifice, guard me, the leader of the sacrifice.
00.02.7 | O Agni, winner of strength, you who are about to run the race, winner of strength, I wipe you clean. Homage to the gods; svadhā to the fathers. May you two be easily guided for me.
00.02.8 | Unspilt today may I gather the clarified butter for the gods; with my foot, O Viṣṇu, may I not tread upon you. May I approach your shadow rich in wealth, O Agni. O Viṣṇu, you are the place; from here Indra did his heroic deed; upright he stood at the rite.
00.02.9 | O Agni, take up the office of hotṛ, take up the office of messenger. May heaven and earth protect you; do you protect heaven and earth. Indra, the maker of good offering for the gods, has been (so) with clarified butter as the oblation; svāhā. Light together with light.
00.02.10 | May Indra place this power in me; may bounteous riches attend us. May the wishes be ours; may our wishes be true. Earth the mother is invoked; may earth the mother invoke me; may Agni from the Āgnīdhra (invoke me); svāhā.
00.02.11 | Heaven the father is invoked; may Heaven the father invoke me; may Agni from the Āgnīdhra (invoke me); svāhā. On the impulse of the god Savitṛ, with the arms of the Aśvins, with the hands of Pūṣan, I receive you; with the mouth of Agni I eat you.
00.02.12 | This sacrifice of yours, O god Savitṛ, they have declared to Bṛhaspati the brahman; by it guard the sacrifice, by it the lord of the sacrifice, by it guard me.
00.02.13 | May mind, the impulse, enjoy the clarified butter; may Bṛhaspati extend this sacrifice; may he set this sacrifice in order, unharmed. May all the gods rejoice here. Om. Stand firm.
00.02.14a | This, O Agni, is your fuel-stick; by it grow and swell. May we too grow and swell. O Agni, winner of strength, you who have run the race, winner of strength, I wipe you clean.

00.02.24a | We have come together with splendour, with milk, with bodies, with an auspicious mind. May Tvaṣṭṛ the good giver bestow riches; may he smooth away what is amiss in the body.

00.02.25 | In the sky Viṣṇu strode out with the jāgata metre; from there excluded is he who hates us and whom we hate. In the mid-air Viṣṇu strode out with the traiṣṭubha metre; from there excluded is he who hates us and whom we hate. On the earth Viṣṇu strode out with the gāyatra metre; from there excluded is he who hates us and whom we hate; from this food, from this foundation. We have reached the light; we have become united with light.
