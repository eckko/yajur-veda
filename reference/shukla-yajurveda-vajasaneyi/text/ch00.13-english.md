00.13.1 | I first take Agni into myself, for the increase of wealth, for good offspring and for good heroic strength; and may the deities attend me.
00.13.2a | You are the back of the waters, the womb of Agni, around the swelling sea; growing, great, spread out in the puṣkara with the measure of the sky and with breadth.
00.13.2b | ~
00.13.3a | Vena, from the boundary and from the front, revealed the first-born brahman and the bright ones; he opened its bases, its nearest points and its stations, and the womb of what is and of what is not.
00.13.3b | ~
00.13.4a | The golden womb came into being at first; born, he was the one lord of what exists. He held up the earth and the sky and this one. To which god may we serve with the oblation?
00.13.4b | ~
00.13.5a | The drop has leapt along the earth and the sky, and along this womb and along the former one. The drop moving along the common womb I offer, following the seven hotrās.
00.13.5b | ~
00.13.6 | Homage to the serpents, whoever they are along the earth; those who are in the airy space, those who are in the sky: to those serpents, homage.
00.13.7 | Those who are the arrows of the sorcerers, or who are along the trees, or who lie in pits: to those serpents, homage.
00.13.8 | Those yonder who are in the bright space of the sky, or who are in the rays of Sūrya, whose seat is made in the waters: to those serpents, homage.
00.13.9a | Make your force like a broad prasiti; go like a mighty king with an elephant. Rushing after the swift prasiti, you are a thrower; pierce the demons with the hottest ones.
00.13.9b | ~
00.13.10a | Your wanderings fly swiftly; touch along, blazing boldly. O Agni, unbound, release with your tongue the hot ones, the flyers, the firebrands, in every direction.
00.13.10b | ~
00.13.11a | Send out the spies in return; be the swiftest, the unharmed guardian of this clan. Whoever the evil-speaker is who is far from us, and whoever is near, O Agni, let no wavering one dare to assail you.
00.13.11b | ~
00.13.12a | Rise up, O Agni; stretch out against them; burn down the enemies, O sharp-weaponed one. O kindled one, whoever has set a hostile power against us, burn him down low, like dry atasa.
00.13.12b | ~
00.13.13a | Be upright, pierce back away from us; make the divine ones manifest, O Agni. Slacken the firm ones of those driven by sorcery; crush the enemies, kin and non-kin. With the splendour of Agni I seat you.
00.13.13b | ~
00.13.13c | ~
00.13.14a | Agni is the head, the summit of the sky, this one the lord of the earth; he quickens the seeds of the waters. With the might of Indra I seat you.
00.13.14b | ~
00.13.15a | You are the leader of bhuvaḥ, of the sacrifice and of the region, where you attend with auspicious yoked teams. In the sky you set your head, light-winning; O Agni, you have made your tongue the carrier of the offerings.
00.13.15b | ~
00.13.16a | You are firm, a support, spread out by Viśvakarman. Let not the sea overpower you, nor the suparṇa; unwavering, make the earth firm.
00.13.16b | ~
00.13.17 | May Prajāpati seat you on the back of the waters, in the īman of the sea; wide, spacious, spread out; you are the earth.
00.13.18a | You are bhū, you are the ground, you are Aditi, all-sustaining, the supporter of the whole world. Hold the earth, make the earth firm, do not harm the earth.
00.13.18b | ~
00.13.19a | For all: for prāṇa, for apāna, for vyāna, for udāna, for firm footing, for conduct. May Agni protect you all around with great well-being, with the most beneficent covering; with that deity, like Aṅgiras, sit firm.
00.13.19b | ~
00.13.20 | Growing forth from joint to joint, from knot to knot; so, O dūrvā-grass, spread forth for us with a thousand and with a hundred.
00.13.21 | You who spread forth with a hundred and grow up with a thousand, of such a you, O goddess, O brick, may we offer service with the oblation.
00.13.22 | O Agni, those lights of yours in Sūrya which stretch over the sky with their rays: with all of them today make us for radiance, for the people.
00.13.23 | O gods, those lights of yours that are in Sūrya, and the lights that are in cows and horses: with all of them, O Indra and Agni, O Bṛhaspati, bestow radiance on us.
00.13.24 | Virāj held the light; Svarāj held the light. May Prajāpati seat you, full of light, on the back of the earth. For all: for prāṇa, for apāna, for vyāna, hold all the light. Agni is your overlord; with that deity, like Aṅgiras, sit firm.
00.13.25a | Madhu and Mādhava, the two seasons of spring; you are the junction within Agni. Let heaven and earth be ordered; let the waters and plants be ordered; let the fires of the same observance be ordered, each for my pre-eminence. The fires that are of one mind between these heaven and earth, being ordered, may they enter the two spring seasons together as the gods enter Indra; with that deity, like Aṅgiras, both of you sit firm.
00.13.25b | ~
00.13.26a | You are unconquered, overcoming; overcome the hostile powers; overcome those who wage battle. You have a thousand powers; such as you are, quicken me.
00.13.26b | ~
00.13.27a | For the one who follows order, the winds are honey; the rivers pour honey; may the plants be sweet for us.
00.13.27b | ~
00.13.28 | Sweet be the night and the dawns; honeyed be the earthly region; sweet be Heaven, our father.
00.13.29 | May the tree be honeyed for us, may Sūrya be honeyed; may the cows be sweet for us.
00.13.30a | Sit in the depth of the waters; let Sūrya not scorch you, nor Agni Vaiśvānara. Look upon the offspring with unbroken leaves; let the divine rain follow after you.
00.13.30b | ~
00.13.31a | The lord of the waters, the bull of the bricks, has moved to the three heavenly seas. Wearing purīṣa, go there, in the world of the well-done, where the former ones have departed.
00.13.31b | ~
00.13.32 | May great Heaven and Earth sprinkle this our sacrifice; may they fill us with their bearings.
00.13.33 | Look upon the rites of Viṣṇu, from where he has beheld the observances; he is Indra's fitting friend.
00.13.34a | You are firm, O support. From here, first from these wombs, Jātavedas was born. May he, knowing, carry the offering to the gods with the gāyatrī, the triṣṭubh and the anuṣṭubh.
00.13.34b | ~
00.13.35 | Delight yourself for nourishment, for wealth, for power, for glory, for sap, for offspring. You are samrāj, you are svarāj; may the two springs of Sarasvatī favour you.
00.13.36 | Harness (them), O Agni, since those horses of yours, O god, which are good, carry sufficiently, for ardour.
00.13.37 | Harness, O Agni, indeed, like a charioteer, the horses that best summon the gods; sit down as the ancient hotṛ.
00.13.38a | They flow together like rivers, like streams, being purified within by heart and mind. I look upon the streams of ghee; a golden reed is in the middle of Agni.
00.13.38b | ~
00.13.39a | You for the verse, you for radiance, you for shining, you for light. This has become the strong one of all the world and of Agni Vaiśvānara.
00.13.39b | ~
00.13.40 | Agni is possessed of light through light, the rukma is possessed of brilliance through brilliance. You are the giver of a thousand; you for a thousand.
00.13.41a | Anoint with milk the Āditya, the embryo, the image of the thousand, of all forms. Spare him with your heat, do not think against him; being piled up, make him of a hundred years of life.
00.13.41b | ~
00.13.42a | The speed of the wind, the navel of Varuṇa, the horse born in the middle of the sarira, the child of the rivers, the tawny one with a mountain for base: O Agni, do not harm him in the highest firmament.
00.13.42b | ~
00.13.43a | I praise with homage Agni, the unceasing drop, the ruddy, swift one, of first thought. He, being arranged joint by joint, season by season: do not harm the cow, Aditi, the virāj.
00.13.43b | ~
00.13.44a | The covering of Tvaṣṭṛ, the navel of Varuṇa, the sheep born from beyond the region, the great thousandfold craft of the asura: O Agni, do not harm it in the highest firmament.
00.13.44b | ~
00.13.45a | The Agni who was born from Agni, from the blaze, from the earth or from the sky; by whom Viśvakarman begot offspring: O Agni, may your anger pass him by.
00.13.45b | ~
00.13.46a | The bright host of the gods has risen, the eye of Mitra, Varuṇa and Agni. Sūrya, the self of what moves and what stands, has filled heaven and earth and the airy space.
00.13.46b | ~
00.13.47a | Do not harm this two-footed animal, you thousand-eyed, being piled for the medha. O Agni, take pleasure in the mayu, the animal, the medha; with that, being piled, settle on the body. May your grief reach the mayu; the one whom we hate, may your grief reach him.

00.13.53a | I seat you in the īman of the waters; I seat you in the udman of the waters; I seat you in the bhasman of the waters; I seat you in the light of the waters; I seat you in the course of the waters; in the flood I seat you in the seat; in the sea I seat you in the seat; in the sarira I seat you in the seat; I seat you in the dwelling of the waters; I seat you in the sadhis of the waters; I seat you in the seat of the waters; I seat you in the common station of the waters; I seat you in the womb of the waters; I seat you in the purīṣa of the waters; I seat you in the pāthas of the waters. 00.13.54a | This one in front is bhuvaḥ; its prāṇa is the bhauvāyana, spring is the prāṇāyana, the gāyatrī is the one belonging to spring. For the gāyatrī the gāyatra, from the gāyatra the upāṃśu, from the upāṃśu the trivṛt, from the trivṛt the rathantara; Vasiṣṭha the sage. By you, taken by Prajāpati, I take prāṇa for the creatures.

00.13.54c | ~
00.13.55a | This one on the south is Viśvakarman; its mind is the vaiśvakarmaṇa, summer is the mānasa, the triṣṭubh is the one belonging to summer; from the triṣṭubh the svāra, from the svāra the antaryāma, from the antaryāma the pañcadaśa, from the pañcadaśa the bṛhat; Bharadvāja the sage. By you, taken by Prajāpati, I take the mind for the creatures.

00.13.56 | This one behind is Viśvavyacas; its eye is the vaiśvavyacasa, the rains are the cākṣuṣya, the jagatī is the one belonging to the rains; from the jagatī the ṛksama, from the ṛksama the bright one, from the bright one the saptadaśa, from the saptadaśa the vairūpa; Jamadagni the sage. By you, taken by Prajāpati, I take the eye for the creatures.
00.13.57 | This one from the north is svar; its ear is the sauva, autumn is the śrautī, the anuṣṭubh is the one belonging to autumn; from the anuṣṭubh the aiḍa, from the aiḍa the manthin, from the manthin the ekaviṃśa, from the ekaviṃśa the vairāja; Viśvāmitra the sage. By you, taken by Prajāpati, I take the ear for the creatures.
00.13.58 | This one above is reflection; for it speech is the mātyā, winter is the vācya, the paṅkti is the one belonging to winter; for the paṅkti the nidhanavat, from the nidhanavat the āgrayaṇa, from the āgrayaṇa the triṇava and trayastriṃśa, from the triṇava and trayastriṃśa the śākvara and raivata; Viśvakarman the sage. By you, taken by Prajāpati, I take speech for the creatures; the world, those, Indra.
