# Shukla Yajurveda: Vajasaneyi Madhyandina Samhita (शुक्लयजुर्वेद: वाजसनेयि-माध्यन्दिन-संहिता)

- Author / compiler: traditional (apaurusheya); Madhyandina recension of the Vajasaneyi (White) Yajurveda, Yajnavalkya tradition
- Subject and purpose of the book: mantras recited by the adhvaryu priest in Vedic rituals (inferred from tradition, not stated in the source file)
- Base edition: not stated by the source; DharmicData file `Yajurveda/vajasneyi_madhyadina_samhita.json`, said to derive from the Vedic Heritage Portal (source S1 in sources.md)
- Mapping: part = none (00), chapter = adhyaya (1 to 40), verse = kandika/mantra as numbered in the source
- Totals: 1 part, 40 chapters, verses to be filled after extraction (the source's highest verse numbers sum to 1974; tradition says about 1,975)

The "Verses (traditional)" column holds, for now, the highest verse number printed in S1 for that adhyaya. It is NOT an independent count. Chapter names are not in the source; "Adhyaya N" is a placeholder and every purpose is inferred.
